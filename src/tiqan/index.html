Adaptive Programs

A plan that builds itself — and adjusts every session.

No spreadsheets, no guessing which progression comes next. Tiqan writes the program from your goal and equipment, then quietly moves it up or down based on your real performance.

  • Push, pull and legs each progress on their own — two strong sessions level you up, two rough ones ease you back
  • Six strength levels per movement, Foundation to Elite, with harder variations swapped in as you climb
  • Equipment-aware — floor only, a pull-up bar, or a full bar-and-rings setup (with a real no-bar pull path)
  • Choose a 3-day full-body or 6-day push / pull / legs split

In the Workout

Guided sets, honest feedback, no nagging.

Timed and rep-based sets, rest timers, get-ready countdowns and tempo cues walk you through the session. After a set you can log how it felt — it's optional, and it quietly shapes what comes next.

  • Rate Form, Tempo and Effort per set — only the toughest signal affects your progression
  • Autoregulation that respects your day: stop an exercise early when a set drops off, or finish the whole session lighter on a rough day
  • Live Activity and Dynamic Island keep your set and rest timer on the Lock Screen
  • Get interrupted? Your workout is restored exactly where you left off
Progress & Insights

See exactly what's working.

Connect Apple Health and Tiqan turns your training into real signal — daily readiness, training load, movement coverage and personal records, all charted from your own data.

Library & Muscle Map

203 exercises, every muscle mapped.

Every movement comes with a demonstration animation, form guidance, and an interactive anatomical map so you can see exactly what you're training — and find your next exercise by tapping a muscle.

  • 203 exercises across push, pull, legs and core — one library for adaptive and custom work
  • Animated demos, target muscles, tempo and step-by-step instructions on every exercise
  • Interactive front / back muscle map on workout cards and exercise sheets
  • Easier and harder neighbours that follow your progression ladder
